OUR INNOVATION

Smart Safety Without CCTV

Customer privacy is important. ShineClean proposes a safety-focused cleaning process without requiring CCTV inside private homes.

Customers can record important valuables, identify restricted rooms and provide special instructions before cleaning begins.

If an item is reported missing, available checklists and service records can support a fair review without automatically blaming the customer or staff.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a cleaning service?

Open the services page, select your required cleaning service and follow the booking steps shown in your account.

How can I check my cleaner's verification?

Visit the Cleaner Verification page. If the status is pending or unavailable, contact support to confirm the details.

Why should I use the Home Inventory checklist?

It helps you record selected belongings and their condition before cleaning. It is a record-keeping tool, not proof that a loss or damage occurred.

How do I report a missing or damaged item?

Open the Complaints page, enter your booking details, explain what you observed and attach a relevant photo if available.

Will my support request be saved?

This demo displays requests during the current page session only. In the complete app, the backend must save requests so customers and authorised admins can access them later.
